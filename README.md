# Digital Marketing & Analytics
Exploring digital marketing through data analysis, sales performance tracking, trend detection, and predictive modeling using Python.

## About the project 
Currently working in **digital and affiliate marketing**, I've become increasingly interested in understanding the impact of data on marketing performance and decision-making.

The sales information I receive is provided through CSV files, containing records of my affiliate marketing activity. However, the available data has its limitations: I don't have full visibility into the individual products being sold or every aspect of customer behavior.

This makes data analysis particularly interesting, as I want to discover how much useful information can be extracted from the available data, identify hidden patterns, understand sales trends, and find opportunities to improve performance.

This project focuses on analyzing affiliate marketing performance, identifying trends and patterns, studying commission dynamics, and exploring predictive models using Python.

## Motivation
Since my work in affiliate marketing has been generating positive results, I want to explore how I can take it further by making more data-driven decisions.

As an engineering student, this project is also an opportunity to apply concepts from programming, statistics, and machine learning to a real-world business scenario.

Ultimately, I want to use technology to identify growth opportunities, optimize marketing strategies, and **increase sales and revenue**.
